Introduction

About this MOOC

Responding to Fire: Language, Action, and Awareness is a self-paced MOOC for B2-level English learners that combines language learning with real-life emergency communication.

It helps learners understand how to react, respond, and communicate effectively in fire-related situations.

Through interactive videos, simulations, and scenario-based tasks, participants strengthen both their vocabulary and civic awareness.

The course is part of the Guardian Angels programme, promoting autonomous learning and global responsibility.

Meet Ember 🔥

Ember is your course guide throughout this MOOC. She will introduce each module, give you tips and cheer you on as you progress through the course. Keep an eye out for her at the start of each section!

Meet Ember — Your Course Guide

The course contains 5 modules

🔍
Discover A series of small games and questions to discover the vocabulary around fire safety. It sets the ambiance for you to engage in the theme and establish keywords important for a smooth learning journey.
🧯
Understand It evokes a few simple yet essential concepts like the different types of fire, the correct ways to extinguish them and a brief training on how to use an extinguisher. It has videos, interactive activities and a few questions to remind you of modal verbs.
🎮
Apply Now that you have the basics down, it's time to play! That's right, in this module, you learn the standard procedure to follow in a fire emergency by playing an escape game and talking to a simulated emergency helpline responder.
🎙️
Analyse In this module, you listen to survivors sharing their testimonies and the possibilities of what could have been through third conditionals. You decode the stories, answer questions and work on grammar.
🏆
Evaluate Based on all the skills and knowledge you've acquired through all the modules, it's now time to test them all through a game mission. The game map presents a set of quizzes and activities to evaluate what you've learned. The course finally ends with a feedback explaining your learning experience throughout the MOOC.
🎖️

You get to play a bunch of fun games while learning and earn a badge and a certificate for completing your course!

Some tips for navigation

  • Use the module tracker at the top of the page to jump between modules at any time.
  • Each module is titled as mentioned above (Introduction, Discover, Understand, Apply, Analyse, Evaluate).
  • Each module has a cover page, and starts with a video of our course guide, Ember, presenting the contents.
  • There are clickable buttons at the end of every module page to go back or move ahead on the course.
